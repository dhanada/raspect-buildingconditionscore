BuildingConditionScore Analytics Methodology

The BuildingConditionScore combines multi-layered architectural telemetry, historical weather stress indexes, regional building permit records, and RaSpect's proprietary Inspectica AI vision benchmark database.

1. Safety Index (40% Weight)

Evaluates facade failure risk, spalling concrete, window frame degradation, and typhoon wind shear stress based on age, construction type, and local storm zones.

2. Serviceability Index (35% Weight)

Calculates moisture ingress probability, sealant shrinkage, thermal movement impact, and tenant environmental comfort scores.

3. Sustainability Index (25% Weight)

Estimates thermal loss through facade envelope, HVAC operational overhead penalty, and carbon footprint alignment with municipal green standards.
