## 3. Detailed Initiatives

### A. Deepen Existing Accounts

**Goal:** Increase revenue per client from one-off inspections to ongoing relationships.

| Action | Details | Revenue Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Inspectica™ SaaS Upsell** | Convert one-time inspection clients to annual platform subscriptions for ongoing monitoring & reporting. | High (recurring) |
| **Periodic Maintenance Contracts** | Offer quarterly/half-yearly re-inspection plans at a retainer. | Medium-High |
| **ESG Reporting Add-on** | Sell ESG metrics & compliance reports as a premium module. | Medium |
| **BIM/GIS Integration Service** | Charge for integrating inspection data into client's existing BIM/GIS systems. | Medium |
| **Multi-building / Portfolio Deals** | Approach existing clients about inspecting their full property portfolio (vs. single buildings). | High |

**Tactics:**
- Assign key account managers to top 20 clients.
- Schedule Q3 business reviews with each to present new offerings.
- Create case studies from existing work to demonstrate ongoing value.

---

### B. Expand Geographically

**Goal:** Enter 2–3 new markets within 12 months.

| Market | Rationale | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Singapore** | Similar high-density urban environment, strong Smart City push, English-friendly. | Partner with local engineering firms; target HDB (public housing) and commercial landlords. |
| **Mainland China (GBA)** | Greater Bay Area initiative — Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Macau. Proximity to HK base. | Joint venture or distributor model; target developers and government bureaus. |
| **UAE / Dubai** | Massive construction boom, high-rise buildings, strong safety regulation. | Exhibitions (CityScape Global); partner with facilities management companies. |
| **UK / Europe** | Mature building safety regulations post-Grenfell; mandatory facade inspections. | Reseller partnership with UK-based surveying firms. |

**Tactics:**
- Hire 1 regional BD lead per target market (commission-heavy comp).
- Attend 2–3 key industry exhibitions per year per market.
- Pursue regulatory certifications (e.g., UKAS accreditation for UK market).

### C. Expand Vertically

**Goal:** Broaden beyond facade inspection into adjacent building/ infrastructure segments.

| Segment | Opportunity | Product Fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Roof Inspection** | Leak detection, wear assessment. | Minor adaptation of drone + AI workflow. |
| **Structural Health Monitoring (SHM)** | Ongoing sensor + visual monitoring of bridges, tunnels, towers. | New offering leveraging AI defect detection. |
| **Construction QA/QC** | In-progress construction quality checks. | Adapt Inspectica™ for construction phase. |
| **Solar Panel Inspection** | Defect detection on solar farms and rooftop panels. | Growing renewables market. |
| **Heritage / Historical Buildings** | Non-invasive condition assessment. | Premium positioning, government-funded. |

**Tactics:**
- Run 2 pilot projects (roof + construction QA) in H2 2026.
- Package as "RaSpect Inspectica™ Multi-Surface."
- Price pilots at cost + minimal margin to build reference cases.

---

### D. Build a Recurring Revenue Engine

**Goal:** Shift from project-based to subscription-based revenue (target: 40% of revenue recurring within 24 months).

| Tier | Offering | Price Point (Monthly) |
|---|---|---|
| **Inspected** | Single building monitoring, quarterly reports | $500–$1,500 |
| **Managed** | Up to 10 buildings, monthly reports, BIM integration | $3,000–$8,000 |
| **Enterprise** | Unlimited buildings, real-time dashboard, dedicated CSM, API access | $10,000+ |

**Features for subscriptions:**
- Continuous AI monitoring from periodic drone flights
- Alerts on new defect detection
- Automated compliance report generation
- Historical trend analysis
- Multi-user dashboard access

**Tactics:**
- Offer 3-month free trial to existing inspection clients.
- Bundle first year of subscription at a discount with new inspection contracts.
- Track churn and NPS rigorously.

### E. Channel Partners & Referrals

**Goal:** Use third-party leverage to acquire clients at lower CAC.

| Partner Type | Why | Deal Structure |
|---|---|---|
| **Facility Management Companies** | Already manage buildings; can resell/ recommend RaSpect. | 15–20% commission on first contract, 10% on renewals. |
| **Engineering / Surveying Firms** | Traditional inspectors who lack AI capability; RaSpect as white-label. | White-label model + rev share. |
| **Drone Service Providers** | Offer data capture; RaSpect provides analysis. | Referral fee or bundled offering. |
| **Insurance Companies** | Insurers want risk data; RaSpect reports help underwrite. | Lead gen partnership. |
| **Property Technology (Proptech) Platforms** | Integration partners (e.g., building management software). | Revenue share on referred conversions. |

**Tactics:**
- Launch "RaSpect Partner Program" in Q4 2026 with tiered benefits.
- Create partner portal with co-branded marketing materials.
- Set up quarterly partner training webinars.

## 8. Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Regulatory hurdles in new markets | Partner with local firms; invest in compliance early |
| Long sales cycles (government/ enterprise) | Build pipeline with smaller commercial clients in parallel |
| Subscription adoption resistance | Offer flexible monthly billing + free trial + strong ROI benchmarking |
| Competitor AI solutions emerging | Accelerate R&D differentiation; lock in clients with data + integrations |
| Talent shortage (sales in new markets) | Use contract BD reps before committing to full-time hires |
