## Executive Summary

RaSpect proposes a focused, hands-on training programme for VTC Hong Kong: a 5-session course (3 hours per session, total 15 hours) that teaches participants how to plan, capture, process and operationalise AI-driven façade inspections using the RaSpect Inspectica platform. The course combines lectures, live demos and practical labs so learners can run end-to-end inspections, interpret AI outputs and generate actionable Building Condition Score reports.

## Course Objectives

- Understand the full Inspectica inspection workflow (data capture → AI inference → scoring → report)
- Operate drones and manage imagery for façade inspection (safety, capture best-practices)
- Interpret model outputs and confidence, and perform human-in-the-loop QA
- Produce standardised Building Condition Score reports and exportable deliverables
- Plan operational rollout and quality assurance for institutional deployment

## Target Audience & Prerequisites

- Target: Technicians, instructors and course leads at VTC; facilities or civil engineering students in applied training tracks.
- Prerequisites: Basic familiarity with building elements and standard safety procedures. Laptop with modern browser; (optional) a sample imagery dataset will be provided for hands-on labs.

## Detailed Programme (Session-by-session)

### Session 1 — Introduction & Platform Overview (3h)
- Topics: Façade safety context, inspection regulations, overview of Inspectica capabilities, evidence chain, data privacy and ethics.
- Demo: Live Inspectica walkthrough — from project creation to report preview.
- Lab: Navigate the Inspectica UI, load a sample project, review sample inference outputs.

### Session 2 — Data Capture & Drone Best Practices (3h)
- Topics: Drone ops for façade inspection, flight planning, image overlap / GSD, lighting considerations, safety checklists and permissions.
- Demo: Uploading and organising imagery, preprocess steps (stitching / tiling basics), metadata management.
- Lab: Prepare and ingest a sample imagery set and verify capture quality in Inspectica.

### Session 3 — AI Models & Defect Detection (3h)
- Topics: How the Inspectica detection models work, labels & taxonomy, confidence scores, common false positives and tuning considerations.
- Demo: Run defect detection on sample imagery, explore bounding boxes, heatmaps, and confidence filters.
- Lab: Review and correct model outputs (human-in-the-loop), create a labelled QC pass.

### Session 4 — Scoring, Reporting & Integration (3h)
- Topics: Building Condition Score methodology, sub-scores (Safety / Serviceability / Sustainability), evidence-based scoring and export formats (PDF, CSV, BIM integration tips).
- Demo: Generate a full Building Condition Score report and walk through the report breakdown.
- Lab: Create a client-ready report from cleaned inspection outputs and export deliverables.

### Session 5 — Operationalisation, QA & Capstone (3h)
- Topics: Scaling inspections across portfolios, scheduling, quality assurance workflows, vendor selection, data governance, and procurement considerations.
- Capstone: Teams run an end-to-end mini-project (ingest → detect → QC → score → report) and present results.
- Closing: Summary, recommended next steps, course feedback & certification of attendance.
