Analytics Methodology

How the BuildingConditionScore is calculated

The BuildingConditionScore is a transparent, desk-based remote assessment built from real public data — OpenStreetMap building attributes, live geocoding, and Open-Meteo climate records — combined into a single, auditable 0–100 health rating. Every penalty is itemised, and data gaps are reported honestly (unknown fields add a small uncertainty penalty rather than a guess).

Index 01 — 40% weight

Safety Index

Evaluates facade failure risk, spalling concrete, window frame degradation, and typhoon wind shear stress based on age, construction type, and local storm zones. Sourced from structural permit records, Inspectica defect benchmarks, and regional climate stress models.

Index 02 — 35% weight

Serviceability Index

Calculates moisture ingress probability, sealant shrinkage, thermal movement impact, and tenant environmental comfort scores — the day-to-day factors that determine whether a building remains fully usable and comfortable.

Index 03 — 25% weight

Sustainability Index

Estimates thermal loss through the facade envelope, HVAC operational overhead penalty, and carbon footprint alignment with municipal green standards — helping you address ESG reporting and energy benchmarks.

Real Data Sources

Nominatim (OSM) — address geocoding
Overpass (OSM) — footprints, height, levels, materials, age
Open-Meteo — 12-month wind, temperature & precipitation
OpenStreetMap tiles — interactive footprint map (Leaflet)

All sources are free and keyless. Where OpenStreetMap has no record for a field (e.g. construction year), the engine reports the gap in "data completeness" and applies a modest uncertainty penalty — it never fabricates values.
